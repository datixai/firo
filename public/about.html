About FIRO

Protecting Kashmir's forests with AI

FIRO (Fire Intelligence & Response Observatory) is a low-cost, real-time wildfire detection system built to run on small devices deep in the forest.

Our mission

Early warning that anyone can afford

Forest fires threaten biodiversity, soil, water and livelihoods across northern Pakistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Most of these forests have no active surveillance, and fires are usually reported only once they are visible from settlements.

FIRO puts AI directly on a small device next to a camera. The device recognises fire on its own, sends a tiny alert to the cloud, and gives forest teams the one thing that matters most in a wildfire: time.

What FIRO sets out to do

  • Detect fire in real time on the device itself, right where the camera is, without depending on the cloud.
  • Achieve reliable detection with high accuracy and low latency for round-the-clock monitoring in changing light.
  • Give response teams a live dashboard and let the public report fires, while sending only small alerts over the network.

Technology

What's inside FIRO

Edge device

A small, low-power computer with a camera that watches the forest and checks every picture on the spot.

On-device AI

AI models trained to recognise fire and smoke with 97.5% accuracy, optimised to answer in under a second.

Secure cloud

Detections and reports are stored securely in the cloud, and only signed-in staff can use the monitoring tools.

Web dashboard

Live map, alert log, analytics and event history. It can be installed on phones like an app.

Alerts

One-tap WhatsApp alerts with coordinates, a Google Maps link and time, in English and Urdu.

Community reports

A public form lets anyone report smoke or fire with GPS location and a photo.

Our journey

From research to the forest

Research

Studied how wildfires are detected today and where current methods fall short.

Building the AI

Trained and compared several AI models and chose the one that best balances speed and accuracy: 97.5%.

Edge deployment

Optimised the AI to run on a low-cost device, answering in under a second.

Cloud & dashboard

Connected the device to the cloud and built the real-time monitoring dashboard with WhatsApp alerts.

Field testing (Dec 2025 to Jan 2026)

Tested on real night-time forest fires near Khuiratta and Nakyal, AJK. Every fire was detected correctly.

Responsible use

FIRO monitors publicly visible forest areas and does not collect personal data from its cameras. Images are processed automatically on the device and used only to detect fire. The system supports early warning and disaster-management authorities. It does not replace human judgement, and every alert should be confirmed before action is taken.
