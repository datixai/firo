About FIRO

Protecting Kashmir's forests with AI

FIRO (Fire Intelligence & Response Observatory) is a low-cost, real-time wildfire detection system built to run on small devices deep in the forest.

Our mission

Early warning that anyone can afford

Forest fires threaten biodiversity, soil, water and livelihoods across northern Pakistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Most of these forests have no active surveillance, and fires are usually reported only once they are visible from settlements.

FIRO puts a trained neural network directly on a Raspberry Pi next to a camera. The device recognises fire on its own, sends a tiny alert to the cloud, and gives forest teams the one thing that matters most in a wildfire: time.

Project objectives

  • Build a real-time, edge-based detection system that captures images with a Raspberry Pi and classifies them on-device, without cloud processing.
  • Achieve reliable detection with high accuracy and low latency for round-the-clock monitoring in changing light.
  • Provide a web dashboard that visualises detections, logs events and supports public fire reporting, sending only compact metadata over the network.

Technology

What's inside FIRO

Edge device

Raspberry Pi 5 with a USB/CSI camera. Captures, preprocesses (224 × 224 RGB) and classifies images locally.

MobileNetV2 Lite

Transfer learning from ImageNet, fine-tuned on 6,247 wildfire images, then INT8-quantised with TensorFlow Lite.

Firebase cloud

Firestore stores detection events and reports. Firebase Authentication protects the monitoring tools.

Web dashboard

Live Leaflet map, alert log, analytics and event history, installable on phones as an app (PWA).

Alerts

One-tap WhatsApp alerts with coordinates, a Google Maps link and time, in English and Urdu.

Community reports

A public form lets anyone report smoke or fire with GPS location and a photo.

Our journey

From dataset to forest

Research & dataset

Reviewed wildfire detection research and prepared a 6,247-image fire / no-fire dataset from Kaggle.

Model training & comparison

Trained and compared six architectures; MobileNetV2 reached 97.5% test accuracy with only 2.6 M parameters.

Edge deployment

Quantised the model to INT8 TensorFlow Lite and deployed it on a Raspberry Pi 5 with sub-second inference.

Cloud & dashboard

Connected the device to Firebase and built the real-time monitoring dashboard with WhatsApp alerts.

Field testing (Dec 2025 to Jan 2026)

Tested on night-time images of real forest fires near Khuiratta and Nakyal, AJK, with 12 out of 12 detected correctly.

Responsible use

FIRO monitors publicly visible forest areas and does not collect personal data from its cameras. Images are processed automatically on the device and used only to detect fire. The system supports early warning and disaster-management authorities. It does not replace human judgement, and every alert should be confirmed before action is taken.
