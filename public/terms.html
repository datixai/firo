1. About FIRO

FIRO is a volunteer wildfire early-warning project from Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Pakistan, developed by Datix AI. The website shares information about forest fires and offers ways to report fires, volunteer, donate and buy FIRO technology.

2. Emergencies

This website is not an emergency service. If anyone is in danger, call Rescue 1122 (Pakistan) or your local emergency number first. FIRO cannot promise that a report sent through the website will be seen or acted on in time.

3. Fire reports

  • Only report fires you honestly believe are real. False or prank reports waste the time of people protecting forests and may be passed to the authorities.
  • Do not put yourself in danger to take a photo or reach a location.
  • You allow FIRO to share your report, including its location and photo, with forest, rescue and disaster-management teams so they can respond.

4. Fire alerts and detection

FIRO's cameras and AI help detect fires early, but no detection system is perfect. Alerts can be missed, delayed or wrong. FIRO supports, and does not replace, forest departments, rescue services and human judgement. Every alert should be confirmed before action is taken.
