Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains what information the FIRO website collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

1. Information you give us

  • Fire reports: what you see, the location (from your device if you allow it, or a pin you place), place description, details, an optional photo, and optionally your name and phone number.
  • Volunteer applications: name, location, email, phone number and your reason for volunteering.
  • Donation pledges: cause, amount and currency, name, email, phone number, and optionally your location and a message.
  • WhatsApp: when you press a WhatsApp button (for example Buy on WhatsApp), WhatsApp opens with a message you can check before sending. Messages you send there are handled by WhatsApp and by us in our chat.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to fires and share reports with forest, rescue and disaster-management teams.
  • To contact volunteers, donors and customers about their request.
  • To publish donation totals and spending. Public totals show only the amount, cause and date, never who gave.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam and misuse.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

Only the FIRO team with admin or control-room access. Fire reports may be shared with the responding authorities. Data is stored with Google Firebase, which processes it for us.

4. Cookies and storage on your device

FIRO does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and has no analytics. The website only stores what it needs to work:

  • Sign-in (Firebase): an anonymous session so forms can be sent securely, and a login session for staff.
  • Preferences: your cookie choice and saved page text so pages load faster.
  • Spam protection: the time of your last report or application, so the same form cannot be sent many times a minute.
  • Offline support: a copy of website files so pages open with a weak connection.

Pages also load fonts, icons and maps from other services (Google Fonts, Cloudflare cdnjs, jsDelivr, Esri and CARTO), which see your IP address as part of normal web traffic. You can change your cookie choice at any time with Cookie settings, and clear stored data in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep it

Reports and applications are kept as long as needed for fire response, records and safety, and then deleted. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us by contacting us on WhatsApp. Name and phone number are optional on fire reports.

7. Children

The website is not aimed at children under 13. Children should ask a parent or teacher before sending any form, except in an emergency.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
